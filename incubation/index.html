Joint industry R&D centres

Companies that run research centres with the university, alongside our faculty.

  • CrisprBits Pvt Ltd

    Bengaluru-based biotechnology company developing CRISPR-based platforms across diagnostics, genome engineering and therapeutics — including PathCrisp for molecular diagnostics of sickle cell disease, typhoid and antimicrobial resistance, and EdiCrisp for gene editing and strain engineering.

    At Atria: Runs the joint Centre of Excellence for CRISPR-based strain engineering and translation.

  • MiniMines Cleantech Solutions Pvt Ltd

    Bengaluru-based cleantech company recovering critical minerals — lithium, cobalt, nickel and copper — from end-of-life lithium-ion batteries using its proprietary Hybrid Hydrometallurgy process, which works across battery chemistries and form factors.

    At Atria: Runs the joint MiniMines–AU Laboratory for Sustainable Resource Recovery, developing biotechnological solutions for metal extraction from waste batteries.

Start-ups working here

Early-stage ventures building new platforms and devices in and around our laboratories.

  • Translatale Biosystems Pvt Ltd

    BIRAC-funded, Bengaluru-based drug-discovery company targeting mRNA translation as a therapeutic entry point. Its platform combines experimental and computational approaches to screen small molecules that modulate ribosome activity and protein synthesis in cancer, neurodegenerative and rare diseases.

  • Aventiq Innovations Pvt Ltd

    BIRAC-funded, Bengaluru-based deep-tech venture building sustainable healthcare technologies, with a focus on safer biomedical-waste management through precision treatment, traceability and responsible disposal.

    At Atria: Developing devices to counter antimicrobial resistance at source through more efficient treatment of biomedical waste.

  • Cogworks

    Lab-automation company building software to drive the laboratory instruments a lab already owns from a phone, by voice, and to rehearse protocols in 3D before a single pipette tip moves.
