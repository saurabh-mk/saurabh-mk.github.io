SynBioAI

SynBioAI brings together curated AI models and workflows for the synthetic biology of molecules and microbes, so scientists can use them with a click rather than stand up hosting infrastructure and model stacks. It is a cross-cutting vertical: its models serve human, crop and planetary health alike, and platform development is driven by specific R&D projects in each theme, and vice-versa.

  3. Peptides with enhanced manufacturability and reduced off-target binding

    SBA3     Most AI peptide-design approaches optimise target affinity alone, while therapeutic development also needs permeability, stability, manufacturability, toxicity and selectivity. Applied to Human Health     Close Details

    Applied to Human Health

    People

    Soham Choudhuri · Praphul Chandra

    Most AI peptide-design approaches optimise target affinity alone, while therapeutic development also needs permeability, stability, manufacturability, toxicity and selectivity. We develop models that jointly consider target binding, manufacturability and reduced binding to structurally similar non-targets.

    Current status

    • Pocket-conditioned generative framework for cyclic peptide design
    • Manufacturability-aware objectives that reduce peptide synthesis liabilities
    • Multi-stage structural screening and ranking with all-atom reconstruction, interaction analysis and strict geometry and clash QC
    • Extending toward reduced off-target binding and family-level selectivity

    Outputs

    • Benchmark results available on request

    Open to

    • Industry partnerships for design and development of peptide applications
    • Experimental collaborations for MD simulation, synthesis, and biochemical and biophysical validation
    • Academic collaborations on AI-driven peptide design and protein–peptide interaction modelling
    • Collaborations on developability — stability, permeability, toxicity and manufacturability prediction

  4. P450 substrate specificity and engineering

    SBA4     Cytochrome P450s are versatile oxidation catalysts, but predicting which P450 will process a given non-natural substrate is the bottleneck in biocatalysis.     Close Details

    People

    Dhruti Patwardhan

    Cytochrome P450s are versatile oxidation catalysts, but predicting which P450 will process a given non-natural substrate is the bottleneck in biocatalysis. A computational pre-screen with even 10–100× enrichment over random screening would sharply cut empirical library screening.

    Current status

    • Evaluating current models on non-natural substrate compatibility for biocatalysis-relevant P450s
    • Developing a model that incorporates physics-based and structural features to fine-tune P450 substrate prediction

    Outputs

    • In progress

    Open to

    • Industry and academic partnerships for use cases needing P450 catalysis predictions
    • Experimental collaborations for MD simulation, synthesis and validation of predicted reactions
