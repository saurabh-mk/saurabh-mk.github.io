Human Health

Cancer spreads and infections persist because of molecular mechanisms we can now study, model and target.

From the molecular pathways that let tumour cells spread, to the efflux pumps and biofilms that let bacteria survive treatment — research that ends in better targets, better therapeutics and better ways to test them. AI-designed peptides from SynBioAI extend the work toward new therapeutics.

Cancer biology

  • Serine hydrolases and their role in metastasis
  • Anti-tumour effects of biogenic nanoparticles and the pathways they affect

Antimicrobial resistance

  • Non-traditional therapeutics to eradicate dual and polymicrobial biofilms

Crop Health

A single bacterial disease can destroy 70–100% of a vegetable crop; sustainable alternatives to chemicals are urgently needed.

Plant disease and the products that prevent it. WILT-SHIELD tackles bacterial wilt — one of the most economically important plant diseases — from early detection to host–pathogen mechanisms and biocontrol, while the agri-biologicals programme develops biopesticides, biostimulants, herbicides and green nanoparticles with agricultural universities and industry.

Plant disease — WILT-SHIELD

  • Early detection of Ralstonia pseudosolanacearum in planta
  • Host- and tissue-specific virulence in Ralstonia
  • Biocontrol of bacterial wilt with beneficial endophytes

Agri-biologicals

  • Microbial formulations for sustainable agriculture
  • Plant essential oil-based nano-emulsion herbicide
  • Green nanoparticles for plant-disease management

Planetary Health

Industrial effluent, e-waste and the loss of biodiversity are among the hardest problems biology can help solve — if it can be engineered to work at scale.

Engineered microbes and enzymes that recover critical metals from waste batteries and treat hazardous industrial effluent; microbial consortia for sewage and faecal sludge; surveillance of resistance in rivers; and the ecology of insect pollinators and biodiversity. Industry-sponsored work here runs through joint centres with CrisprBits and MiniMines.

Resource recovery

  • Bioleaching of precious metals from lithium-ion batteries
  • Synthetic biology for sustainable metal recovery

Effluent and waste treatment

  • Biological treatment for chemical-industry effluents
  • Microbial consortia engineering for faecal sludge and sewage treatment

Environment and One Health

  • Environmental AMR profiling and spatiotemporal river-microbiome analysis

Biodiversity

  • Environmental change and pollinator behaviour
