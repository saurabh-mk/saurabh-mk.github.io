SynBioHub: AI-guided synthetic biology

The Centre’s synthetic biology hub brings an AI models platform, a wet lab and applied research together to solve difficult problems in planetary and human health.

Go to synbiohub.atriauniversity.ai SynBioAI platform

SynBioAI. SynBioLabs. SynBioResearch.

Designed to work together, and accessible independently.

  • SynBioAI

    An AI models platform for the synthetic biology community: target selection, AI predictions and optimisation, with easy access to top models and workflows for researchers and industry.

    Open the platform

  • SynBioLabs

    A microbiology, genetic-engineering and fermentation laboratory that enables data integration, validation and translation. Also the home of the joint centres and the incubation facility.

    Labs & incubation

  • SynBioResearch

    Applied research that generates IP and impact through industrial partnerships — industrial effluent treatment and microbial lithium-battery recycling, part of the Centre’s planetary-health theme.

    Planetary health

A tight design–build–test loop

AI proposes, the wet lab tests, and the results feed back into the models.

  1. 01

    Design

    AI models propose enzymes, peptides and strains.

  2. 02

    Build

    The wet lab constructs and grows the candidates.

  3. 03

    Test

    Experiments measure what worked.

  4. 04

    Learn

    Results feed back to the models, so each cycle is sharper.

Enzymes, peptides and strains

Capabilities available or being added to SynBioAI today.

  • Enzyme discovery from reactions
  • AI/ML-assisted enzyme improvement
  • Protein and peptide binder design
  • Protein and peptide property screening
  • Automated agentic model and pipeline deployment

Access is currently free but limited. Enzyme discovery and engineering and protein and peptide design and screening are available; strain discovery, screening and engineering workflows are being added.
