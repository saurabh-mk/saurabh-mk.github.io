Six engagement models

Choose the one that fits, or combine them. We are happy to co-define the problem with you.

Sponsored & joint research

Co-defined, IP-generating R&D with a clear path to application — the Centre-of-Excellence model, as in our joint centres with CrisprBits and MiniMines.

Consulting & advisory

Expert opinion, feasibility studies and scoped technical projects from our faculty.

Lab-as-a-Service

Molecular, microbiological and cell-culture laboratories, with equipment and scientists, for defined pieces of work.

Platform-as-a-Service

Curated AI models and workflows for enzymes, peptides and strains through SynBioAI, without standing up model infrastructure.

Incubation

Space, lab access, early platform access and AI consulting for start-ups working in synthetic biology and adjacent fields.

Training & education

Project-based courses, internships and tailored training for teams — from systems biology to AI for biology.

Industry-run centres inside the university

Two companies run joint centres at Atria, embedding their scientists alongside our faculty.

Centre of Excellence in Strain Engineering and Translation

A joint centre pairing CrisprBits’ genome-editing and strain-engineering expertise with the Centre’s AI-guided design and wet-lab capability. Active programme: biological treatment of chemical-industry effluents.

MiniMines–AU Laboratory for Sustainable Resource Recovery

A joint laboratory developing biotechnological solutions for extracting metals from waste lithium-ion batteries. Active programme: isolation of leaching microbes and bioleaching media optimisation.

From first call to delivery

  1. 01

    Conversation

    A short call to understand the problem and whether biology can help.

  2. 02

    Scoping

    A written scope: question, approach, deliverables, timeline and cost.

  3. 03

    Agreement

    A simple agreement covering IP, confidentiality and publication.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Faculty-led work with regular reviews, ending in a report, data and, where relevant, a prototype.
