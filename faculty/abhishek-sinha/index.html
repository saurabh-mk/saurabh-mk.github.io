Dr. Abhishek Sinha

Associate Professor, Life Sciences

Abhishek investigates the molecular pathways that drive cancer metastasis and applications of nanotechnology to cancer. His group combines proteomics and computational analysis with in vitro work, and seeks clinical and industry partners for tissue-level validation and first-line drug studies.

Current research

  • Serine hydrolases and their role in metastasis
  • Anti-tumour effects of biogenic nanoparticles and the pathways they affect
