Dr. Kristi Kabyashree

Assistant Professor, Life Sciences

Kristi’s research spans bacterial genetics, pathogen–host interactions, microbial antagonism and plant immunity, with the aim of sustainable management of crop disease. Her doctoral work established host-dependent virulence and tissue-specific colonisation in Ralstonia; her postdoctoral work studied beneficial endophytes and plant defence.

Email Google Scholar ORCID

Current research

  • Early detection of Ralstonia in planta
  • Host- and tissue-specific virulence of Ralstonia pseudosolanacearum
  • Biocontrol of bacterial wilt with beneficial endophytes
